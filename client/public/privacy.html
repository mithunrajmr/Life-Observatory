Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 • Life Observatory

At Life Observatory ("we", "our", or "us"), your personal privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental architectural principles. Life Observatory is designed as a personal longitudinal reflection instrument that helps you observe gradual shifts and personal growth over time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our application at https://life-observatory-app-970088269582.us-central1.run.app, how that information is processed, and your complete rights to control, export, or permanently delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver longitudinal reflection and insights:

  • Account Credentials: When you sign in via Google OAuth or Firebase Authentication, we receive your email address, display name, user ID, and profile picture avatar.
  • User Self-Reflections: Text journal reflections, personal milestones, turning points, and goals that you explicitly submit to the observatory.
  • Connected Life Instrument Metadata: When you voluntarily connect external Google Workspace accounts (such as Google Calendar, Gmail, or Google Drive), our system accesses metadata signals only (such as meeting duration, messaging cadence, and document creation timestamps). We never access, read, or store the contents or body of your private emails, document text, or calendar attendee lists.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To compute your multi-domain longitudinal trajectories across Career, Health, Learning, Relationships, Energy, and Personal milestones.
  • To surface invisible progress and subtle momentum shifts across monthly and quarterly reflection windows.
  • To power the private, on-demand AI thinking companion (powered by Google Gemini) using only your authenticated session context.
  • To maintain authenticated security and prevent unauthorized access to your records.
Google API Services User Data Policy Disclosure Life Observatory's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Limited Use & Third-Party Transfer Safeguards

We believe your personal life narrative belongs exclusively to you:

  • Zero Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal data or Google user data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • No Ad Targeting: Your data is never used to serve advertisements or personalize advertising profiles.
  • No Foundation Model Training: Your private reflections and Google user data are never used to train or fine-tune generalized commercial artificial intelligence or machine learning models without your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage, Isolation & Security

Life Observatory runs on Google Cloud Run with strict end-to-end encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) and at rest. Your reflections and timeline events are stored in user-isolated Google Cloud Firestore subcollections protected by granular database security rules matching your unique authenticated user identifier (UID).

5. Data Retention & Hard Deletion Rights

You maintain complete sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • Export: You may view and review your complete timeline and reflection history at any time.
  • Permanent Purge: You can initiate an immediate, complete hard deletion of your account and all associated reflections directly in the application under Settings > Data & Privacy > Erase All Data. All records are permanently erased from Firestore within seconds.
